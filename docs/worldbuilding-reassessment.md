# Worldbuilding reassessment after the magic correction

Status: **governing revision ledger**. This document records which accumulated
assumptions remain usable after Maldirin's magic stopped being defined primarily
by environmental situatedness. It does not settle the open questions listed
below. When an older consultation or proof conflicts with this ledger and the
canonical documents it cites, the older material is historical.

## Why the revision was necessary

The project began from a sound requirement: powers should have intelligible
bases, NPCs should obey their own limits, and environments should matter when
they materially interact with an act. Repeated ideation gradually strengthened
that requirement into a much narrower identity:

1. nearly every capability acquired a local enabling condition;
2. exceptional practitioners became dependent on providers rather than carrying
   exceptional capacity themselves;
3. regions were designed as networks of magical permissions;
4. class candidates were praised for establishing their own local conditions;
   and
5. central conflicts repeatedly converged on actors who severed, translated,
   moved, monopolized, or invalidated those conditions.

That attractor produced coherent puzzles but reduced magical breadth, portable
player agency, direct action, and surprise. It also made unrelated cosmologies
feel like variations of one infrastructure problem.

The correction is not “environment never matters.” It is:

> Magic has a source, a method, and consequences. Place matters when the power,
> practitioner, target, or act gives it reason to matter.

See [Magic: sources, methods, and consequences](magic-and-environment.md) for the
normative contract.

## Stable foundations

The following survive the correction and remain the strongest current
foundation:

- Maldirin is the world or broader setting and is probably multi-continental.
  The first campaign begins in one contiguous continental theater.
- The theater contains old, asymmetric, and overlapping powers rather than one
  recent collapse or universal sovereign.
- The great decline is ancient. Present societies have deep histories of their
  own; old works and precursor peoples do not make them temporary squatters.
- The age is undergoing uneven movement, ambition, exploitation, renewal,
  contact, reclamation, and degradation for many independent reasons.
- There is no common aspiration. Many people seek something worth inheriting,
  while others seek survival, release, mastery, conquest, knowledge, pleasure,
  or ends that admit no successor.
- Magic is uncommon specialized capacity; necromancy is a rare subset. Mundane
  work and non-necromantic magic remain important.
- Gods and Gods exist in genuinely different magnitudes and forms. Their agency
  is inherent. Their limits are individual truths proportionate to what they
  are, not one constitution governing all divinity and not a universal need for
  mortal sites, worship, or aspects.
- The primordial foundation is established: the First knew itself in surprise
  and answered existence with gratitude. Existence was good, so the First gave
  being onward as lives genuinely their own. Otherness is a positive creation,
  not a limit discovered or imposed upon the First.
- Ordinary reality is dependable. The supernatural can be exceptional,
  invasive, alien, or world-changing without making every road dream logic.
- World facts, actors, institutions, powers, anomalies, and generated content
  require provenance even when that provenance is not player knowledge.
- Actions, evidence, beliefs, claims, relationships, standing, and pressure
  remain separate state. The world persists between testaments.
- Superversive outcomes are possible but not guaranteed. Cost, tragedy,
  compromise, refusal, and failure remain real without a plot clock forcing the
  player toward one approved resolution.

## Corrected or reopened foundations

### Magic

Old formulation: practitioners arrange relationships that Maldirin answers, and
capability is principally evaluated through local conditions and providers.

Current formulation: different arts may arise from trained or altered selves,
learned techniques, Spirit, matter, preparations, artifacts, other persons,
relationships, targets, collectives, time, place, or stranger sources. Each act
declares its own basis, method, costs, affinities, limits, and counters. Hard
environmental requirements are specific and uncommon; portable agency is normal
for many specialists.

Consequence: regional design should explain who is trained, supplied, altered,
patronized, welcomed, or constrained there—not reduce every practitioner to the
site tag currently underneath them.

### Divinity

Old formulation: gods and Gods were too often described like mortal specialists
whose effective action depended upon the correct site, worship, aspect, or
relationship.

Current formulation: divine agency is inherent. Some gods are local because
their actual personhood is a hearth, river, bridge, lineage, species, or other
bounded reality; others travel, divide, or act at a distance. Gods may work
across lands, ages, worlds, species, and orders of existence. Worship, temples,
bodies, aspects, covenants, and agents can give a divine power precision,
persistence, intimacy, leverage, legibility, or vulnerability. They do not
universally provide permission or fuel.

Consequence: divine limits must be truths commensurate with the being—rivals,
incompatible orders, divided attention, internal contradiction, self-binding,
ancient defeat by comparable power, catastrophic cost of full action, or an
intrinsic incapacity. Mortals may defeat an aspect, expose or sever an agent,
frustrate an intervention, exploit a manifestation or rival, and make a course
too costly without thereby reducing the whole God to a disabled local machine.

### Cosmology and the central conflict

The primordial foundation now lives in
[Cosmogenesis](cosmogenesis.md). Surprise is not treated as evidence of the
First's ignorance or weakness; the First did not contain or command later lives
as the condition of their being, rather than being unable to do so. The number
of first Others, their ability to give being onward, and the formation of
sequence, substance, the Vast, Gods, worlds, and Maldirin remain open.

The plurality of Maldirin's settled order, the Vast's many possible orders, and
the varied later origins of gods remain useful provisional cosmology. Dominion
on the March is no longer the preferred flagship by default. Nymorai, Siege
Sovereign, Moving Shade, Interdict, Celestial Annexation, Closed Country, and
other surviving concepts are candidates with different production risks.

Any future prime mover must be reassessed without granting automatic value to
the control of magical conditions. “It changes who may use which local power”
can be one consequence; it is not sufficient campaign spark or a mandatory
engine. The mover should possess an intelligible will or causal force, direct
stakes worth caring about, recurring embodied or personal opposition, and a
world that continues to produce independent regional stories.

### Regions and procedural generation

Regions still orient population. A water-working lineage plausibly clusters
around the water it serves; a court attracts the arts it sponsors; a persecuted
practice migrates; a portable military art travels with armies. This is social,
historical, economic, bodily, and supernatural causality—not an instruction to
give every NPC a locality requirement.

Generated specialists should be assembled from coherent capability packages:

- source and acquisition history;
- training, bodily alteration, patronage, or other basis;
- carried equipment and preparations;
- costs, exhaustion, injuries, and capacity;
- actual requirements and available substitutes;
- favored affinities and known counters;
- social role, motive, travel history, and reason for being present; and
- credible fallback when currently deprived.

Environmental providers remain useful for capabilities that truly use them.
They are one optional part of the package.

### Economy and surplus

Grounded production, scarcity, inequality, maintenance, access, and local
variance remain desirable. A supernatural surplus engine can raise one town or
network above subsistence without uplifting an entire region. Its effects follow
capacity, catchment, ownership, transport, skilled labor, and degradation.

The correction broadens what such an engine can be. It need not be an
environmental permission source for local magicians. It may instead produce
food, heat, transport, durable materials, healing, prediction, safe storage,
labor, or something culturally valuable. Portable practitioners, trade,
institutions, ordinary craft, conquest, and inherited capital can spread or
contest its benefits. The game may let a player engage, exploit, reform, seize,
or ignore an economy; it must not become mandatory small-business simulation.

### Language and standardization

The layered hybrid remains the working direction: a trade and administrative
common exists across part of the first theater, while many languages have
histories unrelated to it. The relationship is society-to-language role, not
“every other language descends from the common.” Isolation, prestige,
administration, migration, conquest, religion, trade, and re-engagement shape
actual repertoires.

The old broad standardizing force that established the common is not yet pinned
down. It may be an empire, ecumene, distributed institution, inherited work, or
another materially exceptional power. Its exceptional advantage must be
designed under the corrected magic contract; universal translation of local
conditions or “Common Ground” is no longer the default answer.

### First-theater regional foundation

The Veyward remains a provisional production scaffold, not canonical geography.
Its grounded variation in society, household meaning, language, dress,
architecture, economy, nonhuman rarity, authority, and visual provenance
survives. Fire Road and Siege Sovereign details remain disposable proof facts.

The region's magical examples now test different degrees of portability rather
than illustrating one situated system. Future scene packets should record which
magic facts are inherited, durable regional proposals, controlled deviations,
or disposable proof details.

## Practice and tradition reassessment

A **practice** remains a useful design term for the repeated object and decision
loop that defines character play. A **tradition** remains a lineage, doctrine,
institution, culture, or method that changes how a practice is learned and used.
Neither taxonomy is necessarily universal in-world.

The corrected magic model strengthens practices whose identity survives without
a locality gate:

| Concept | Source and method under review | Repeated player object | Environmental role | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | a trained or altered living capacity admits dead initiative under negotiated or dangerous control | the shared living body and its guests or faculties | particular dead, sites, or rites may change whom one can admit; the baseline travels with the Medium | strong provisional core |
| Bonebinder | prepared remains are made capable of carrying durable initiative and maintained as a second body | one corporeal servant, its anatomy, equipment, history, and orders | workshops and materials enable major reconstruction; ordinary direction remains portable | strong provisional core |
| Renderer | learned transformations expose and prepare properties in death-altered matter | a limited kit of preparations and material reactions | local matter changes ingredients and reactions; prepared competence travels | strong provisional core |
| Summoner | prepared calls, relationships, remains, or another carried basis coordinate a bounded external formation | formation, cohesion, standing orders, composition, and duration | local dead and violent history alter opportunities; they do not supply the only usable formation | strong provisional concept; needs implementation proof |
| Mortality practice (working concept) | a trained or altered practitioner arrests and recalls their own living functions, then commits that state through martial action | the practitioner's own vital state and safe or failed return | mortuary tools, remains, sites, and instruction may shape techniques; the altered body carries the baseline | serious fifth-seat candidate; needs direct-play proof |
| Grave Warden / Cairnwright | prepared mortuary fittings currently make visible boundaries, but the affected substance and portable basis remain unclear | bounded ground and physical lines | death-altered sites may shape or amplify a valid act; they cannot substitute for defining it | reopened; no reserved roster seat |
| Ferryman | a real mode of dead passage is conducted, denied, or reversed | passage and defeat transitions | the mode may be regional, divine, artifact-bound, or otherwise specific | regional/advanced unless broader proofs earn portability |

### Traditions that remain promising

- **Medium — Guest House / Borrowed Faculty:** recognizable guests and negotiated
  habitation oppose the extraction of useful fragments. Both rely on a changed
  living practitioner, not an ambient provider.
- **Bonebinder — Conservator / Compositor:** one preserves or carefully uplifts a
  body's inherited form; the other plans, grafts, and distorts a macabre golem
  for future needs. Their mutual distaste is culturally and mechanically fertile.
- **Renderer — Refiner / Cultivator:** one isolates reliable properties; the
  other maintains active transformations. Place and time can matter more to the
  Cultivator without turning either tradition into environmental magic by
  definition.
- **Summoner — Retinue / Levy:** one maintains a few recognizable shades; the
  other forms temporary collective impressions. Both need a portable baseline,
  sequential unit action, order-then-target control, and visible cohesion.
- **Mortality practice — Last Vigil / Pale Discipline:** one regards complete
  return from a bounded deathward state as the discipline's proof; the other
  accepts longer suspension and lasting bodily change. Both must remain martial
  practices of weapons, anatomy, wounds, and recall rather than generic pale
  spell packages.

### Concepts requiring fresh work

- Grave Warden/Cairnwright needs a source-method-consequence account before more
  abilities. “Continuity,” “custody,” and “breach” are too broad; portable stakes
  are readable equipment but not yet an explanation.
- Ferryman must not canonize one universal afterlife or become teleportation with
  funerary nouns. Regional traditions remain valid and potentially powerful.
- The mortality practice is the current fifth-seat candidate because it owns a
  distinct repeated object: the practitioner's own vital state. It still earns
  nothing until direct play proves suspend, commit, fight, and return as fair,
  interesting decisions rather than coefficients or deferred-damage bookkeeping.
- Martial formation is cross-practice. Revenancy is a rare campaign
  transformation preserving the original practice, not a normal starting class.
  Lichdom remains the greater achievement of durability beyond one body. See
  [Martial necromancy and Revenancy](martial-necromancy-and-revenancy.md).
- The implemented Grave Warden remains save-compatible prototype content. Its
  presence in code is not a worldbuilding vote.

## Historical material and citation discipline

- Raw consultations and panel dossiers record the process. Do not silently
  rewrite them as though an agent never proposed an obsolete assumption.
- `world.md`, `magic-and-environment.md`, `testament-continuity.md`, and this
  ledger govern current cross-cutting decisions.
- `first-theater-regional-foundation.md` governs provisional vertical-slice
  consistency only within its explicit A/B/C/D status system.
- `practices.md` records implementation compatibility and current conceptual
  status; `class-and-tradition-ideation.md` contains the latest detailed class
  material but remains provisional.
- Central-conflict dossiers are candidates, not cosmology. A proof may establish
  that its own God, empire, magic, or species exists without establishing a
  universal rule for Maldirin.

## Decisions to make next

1. Define the first theater's historical standardizing force and the exceptional
   advantage that once gave its administrative common broad reach.
2. Re-evaluate the strongest central-conflict candidates under direct-play
   requirements and the corrected magic contract.
3. Write source/method/consequence sheets for Medium, Bonebinder, Renderer,
   Summoner, and the mortality-practice candidate before expanding their skill
   trees.
4. Either produce a defensible necromantic basis for Cairnwright or demote its
   spatial techniques to equipment, traditions, and authored scenes.
5. Prototype the mortality practice's martial loop and paper-test one failed and
   one successful Revenant transformation through testament continuity.
6. Seek additional practice concepts without requiring a fixed roster size in
   advance.
7. Build one quiet, inhabited regional location to test language, economy,
   recurring characters, services, and social memory outside catastrophe.
8. Update procedural capability data gradually from environmental-condition
   language to generic contextual requirements and affinities while preserving
   deterministic behavior and save compatibility.
