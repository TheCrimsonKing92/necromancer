# Advancement runway

Status: implementation boundary and paper roadmap, July 2026.

## Current playable runway

The XP curve reaches level 4 at 105 total experience. The implemented graph now
has three practice tiers for each playable prototype, three shared body tiers,
three shared necromancy tiers, and independent mundane training roots.

The level-4 practice nodes deliberately alter cadence:

- Medium turns Ward earned through Spirit Lash into protection from Borrowed
  Might’s bodily strain.
- Summoner chooses between renewing a shade and letting its final command fire
  when the binding expires.
- Renderer chooses between preserving Corpsefire’s damage over time and
  collapsing it into a Grave Salt strike.

Renderer also has a separate Level II residue decision: unfinished Corpsefire
can be decanted into bounded Spirit or carried into another standing enemy.
Bonebinder's Level III Set the Frame discipline raises Ward on the servant's
first actual Interposition each round; selecting or changing an order alone
cannot create protection.

The shared level-4 nodes remain bounded. Last Stance raises Brace’s threshold
only below half Health and cannot stack or restore Spirit. Death Dividend
converts otherwise-wasted defeat recovery at no more than 2 Ward per enemy and
never exceeds the Ward limit.

Mundane training currently advances Medicine, Survival, or Barter directly.
These ranks already change treatment odds, authored checks, and exchange terms;
later nodes should add qualitative approaches only when their scenes or
transitions exist end to end.

## Deferred nodes that must not become selectable yet

### Medium guest disciplines

Do not add guest-selection nodes until named guests and literal terms of
hospitality are represented as typed practice state. The intended later forks
are reciprocal hospitality, forceful occupation, and a limited chorus. Each
must change available actions, bodily risk, and at least one social approach.
Copy alone is not an implementation.

### Renderer preparation disciplines

Do not add preparation-slot, substitution, harvesting-quality, or provenance
nodes while Renderer remains the playable compatibility prototype.
Renderer nodes require typed preparation slots, baseline refills that do not
create harvesting chores, and an authored route for material quality and
evidence. White Quench is compatible with the current action pair; it is not a
substitute for that foundation.

### Summoner succession

The current Summoner line remains supported for existing testaments. Do not
extend it into anonymous disposable-minion scale. If Bonebinder assumes the
persistent-companion role, preserve save compatibility and decide explicitly
which Summoner disciplines migrate, remain legacy-only, or become later
Bonebinder shade techniques.

## Next paper pass

Before level 5 content, define branch forks rather than another linear row.
Candidate forks must state the recurring decision they create, the exact rule
primitive that implements it, its Witness and action-card disclosure, and the
mixed encounter cases used to test it. Avoid pure damage coefficients unless
they support an otherwise meaningful conversion or tradeoff.
