# Independent game concepts

## Purpose

This document preserves strong game premises that should not be distorted to fit
*Necromancer*. Admission here is not rejection. It means the concept's most
distinctive player role, verbs, or campaign structure would make necromancy
incidental, artificially privileged, or actively limiting.

Nothing in this document is Maldirin canon. A future project may inherit a
concept's causal structure without inheriting Maldirin, its cosmology, its names,
or its terminology.

## Admission test

Move or copy a concept here when most of the following are true:

- It suggests a strong player identity other than “a necromancer in a fallen
  world.”
- Its best repeated actions would remain nearly unchanged if necromancy were
  removed.
- Making necromancy central would narrow or misrepresent its premise.
- Its mechanics could support a full game rather than merely one region, quest,
  antagonist, or optional system.
- It has a distinct emotional or strategic appeal worth preserving through later
  development.

Each entry should record what must survive, why it fits poorly, its likely game
loop, its principal risks, and its source. Do not merge entries merely to reduce
their number.

## Committed concept

### Species-Scale Annexation

**Premise.** A foreign civilization has no individual-scale citizens. Its
political persons are whole species. It enters an inhabited biosphere by inducing
coordinated agency among existing plants and animals, recognizing each species
as a prospective constituent, and refusing to recognize individual humans,
villages, or states as valid diplomatic parties.

**Why it deserves its own game.** Its natural subject is personhood across scale:
who may speak for a species, whether political awakening liberates or conscripts
individual organisms, and what becomes of predation, cultivation, domestication,
and ecological dependence when populations acquire political standing. A
necromancer could participate, but death magic supplies neither the necessary
player identity nor the central verbs. Privileging it would shrink the premise.

**Likely player role.** A mobile individual or small delegation anomalously able
to negotiate across individual, community, and species scales. The player need
not represent humanity faithfully and may eventually acquire authority from
more than one kind of political person.

**Potential game loop.** Investigate newly coordinated behavior; determine what
body or assembly presently speaks for a species; intervene in conflicts between
individual welfare and population policy; negotiate, sabotage, shelter, hunt,
cultivate, or form coalitions; revisit changed ecologies whose political
relationships persist after the player leaves.

**What must survive.**

- The foreign civilization genuinely thinks and acts at species scale.
- Existing life is politicized; it is not replaced by foreign biomass.
- Individual minds are not erased into a conventional hive mind.
- Human institutions are not automatically recognized as humanity's legitimate
  voice.
- Agriculture, husbandry, predation, disease, and symbiosis become political
  without becoming a spreadsheet of ecological services.

**Primary risks.** Abstraction, accidental comedy, reducing species to human
factions in costume, ecological-management tedium, and allowing one convenient
avatar to collapse the scale problem back into ordinary diplomacy.

**Provenance.** Fresh objective-first Sol ideation, 2026-08-11; moderator screen
in `.panel/objective-nature-adversarial-and-fresh-screen-2026-08-11.md`.

## Strong candidates for independent development

These concepts have substantial promise but have not yet received the same
explicit commitment as Species-Scale Annexation.

### Army Behind Sleep

**Premise.** Contact with a foreign commander conscripts initiative into a
persistent second theater fought during sleep. A character's waking body remains
in Maldirin while an authored portion of that character acts on the foreign
front. Choices about when and where to sleep determine which body is exposed,
which theater advances, and which relationships can be maintained.

**Why it may be a separate game.** Its signature is a coupled-world campaign:
two vulnerable bodies or states, two fronts, and deliberate allocation of
presence. That structure wants every class, settlement, save system, and story
arc designed around divided agency. Making it one practice available to a
necromancer would waste the premise; making it mandatory would redefine
*Necromancer* around dream warfare.

**What must survive.** Both theaters remain material and causal; dream logic
cannot excuse arbitrary outcomes. Neglect has persistent consequences in either
theater. Sleeping is a strategic commitment rather than a cutscene trigger.

**Primary risks.** Two campaigns that never feel equally real, opaque dream
causality, exhausting context switching, and optimal play degenerating into
sleep-schedule management.

**Provenance.** `.panel/prime-wave-sol-wildcard-2026-08-10.md`.

### The Maker of the Unrepeatable

**Premise.** A supreme living maker turns the magic that made her work dependable
against dependable imitation itself. Copied excellence begins to fail when its
neglected conditions no longer hold; worthy action must be reinvented in its
actual context.

**Why it may be a separate game.** Its best play is about craft, pedagogy,
tradition, authorship, apprenticeship, and the difference between living practice
and prestigious repetition. Necromancy can supply one poignant technique—such as
a willing dead hand guiding a motion fixed to a particular ended relationship—but
it should not own the game's answer.

**Likely player role.** A practitioner, investigator, or leader assembling a
working tradition from teachers, rivals, local materials, failed exemplars, and
new conditions rather than collecting universal recipes.

**What must survive.** Failures must be predictable from observable conditions;
the game cannot arbitrarily declare that imitation is bad. Ancient communal
practice may remain alive through unnoticed variation, while fashionable novelty
can freeze into repetition within weeks.

**Primary risks.** Enormous authored-content burden, rules that feel capricious,
and turning a difficult material conflict into an essay about originality.

**Provenance.** `.panel/contrastive-ideator-sol-xhigh-2026-08-09.md` and
`.panel/last-two-rounds-strengthening-sol-xhigh-2026-08-09.md`.

### The Fifth Curtain

**Premise.** A refugee performance divinity uses an unfinished drama to establish
the material conditions under which it and its people can have a future. As the
play is performed, coerced roles and unresolved scenes become real conditions in
the surrounding world.

**Why it may be a separate game.** Its strongest mechanics would concern casting,
rehearsal, refusal, interpretation, public performance, and the consequences of
who is forced or permitted to inhabit a role. Those mechanics deserve to be the
game's foundation rather than a supernatural episode in a necromancer's journey.

**What must survive.** The divinity has a concrete future-directed objective;
performance changes material conditions rather than merely symbolizing them;
participants retain the ability to resist, reinterpret, or abandon roles; later
play expands beyond repeatedly staging another scene.

**Primary risks.** Representation-becomes-reality is already a crowded idea,
theatrical language can obscure causality, and the campaign can collapse into a
sequence of performances whose consequences are decided by authorial fiat.

**Provenance.** `.panel/prime-wave-sol-wildcard-2026-08-10.md`.

## Candidate fragments, not complete games

Fragments may be recorded here when they are excellent but do not yet imply a
whole independent game. Examples currently worth retaining include:

- A created work that can speak only through the gifts it contains and uses those
  borrowed voices to refuse its maker.
- A hospice keeper's principle that “the dying give too easily,” making consent
  under terminal pressure a concrete institutional problem.
- Victory restoring guilds or institutions that then suppress the improvisers
  whose work made victory possible.

These fragments do not inherit the surrounding candidate automatically. They
remain available to a future project only after a new causal and gameplay context
is established.

## Maintenance rule

When *Necromancer* ideation produces a compelling premise with the wrong player
center, preserve it here before rejecting it. Record the reason for separation
plainly. Do not use this shelf as a dumping ground for merely strange, unfinished,
or weak pitches.
